Voice

Want a nicer voice? On your Samsung, open Settings, search "Text-to-speech", set the preferred engine to Google, then tap its gear → Install voice data → English. Pick the new voice here after.

What you can say

Tap the green mic, then say:

  • "Morning" or "Brief me": your brief
  • "What's waiting": everything that needs you
  • "What's running" or "What's done"
  • "Balkan Power", "Marsilda", "Besa" or a thread name: where it stands
  • "Open" + a thread name: shows it, one tap to go there
  • "Remind me to …": adds a to-do
  • "My to-dos", "What's new", "Goodnight"
  • "Stop": stops talking

No sound on your Samsung?

Settings → Notifications → App notifications → Chrome (or Assistant) → Notification categories → xgertio4.github.io → Alert, and pick a sound. Also turn off Settings → Notifications → Advanced settings → Notification cooldown.
